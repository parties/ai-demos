AI demos for school and district staff

Interactive examples of what AI does to the work that repeats. 30 demos, grouped by who does the work. Every record is fabricated; every page works offline.

Everyone

The four-part request

Verb, format, voice, context: add one part at a time and watch a board memo, a family letter or a job posting go from generic to usable.

Where it fails, and how you catch it

Three finished-looking answers with planted mistakes; one click shows each flaw and the habit that catches it.

Make it ask you questions first

The same request asked plainly, then with five questions first: a template full of blanks versus a letter you can send.

Superintendent

Board memo first drafts

Bullet notes plus last month's memo become a draft in the board's house format, with uncertain figures flagged.

Board packet in one page

A 148-page public packet becomes decisions due, dollars, and the items likely to draw questions, each with a page number.

Evidence before a decision

The documents on a decision become a sourced pro/con brief, where they conflict, and the questions still to ask.

Rehearse a hard meeting

AI plays a skeptical board member, an upset parent, or a reporter, and coaches each answer.

Human capital and HR

Job postings, flagged and fixed

A duties list becomes a posting, with jargon and requirements that shrink the applicant pool flagged and rewritten.

Interview kits, same for everyone

One posting becomes structured questions and a scoring rubric identical for every candidate. Humans screen; AI never ranks.

The August question flood

A dense benefits packet becomes the 20 questions HR answers every year, each answer citing its section.

Contract language, read closely

A clause in plain English, or every contradiction between the handbook and a negotiated agreement, side by side.

Information services

Vendor privacy review

A vendor's privacy policy checked against the district's own list, every finding tied to a quoted line. The decision stays yours.

Repeat help-desk tickets

A technician's terse fix notes become a numbered how-to any staff member can follow.

Change and outage notices

A jargon-heavy maintenance plan becomes a calm staff notice: what changes, when, and what to do.

Spreadsheet formulas

Describe a result and get the formula, or paste an inherited formula or script and get it explained line by line, risks included.

Curriculum and instructional technology

Standards coverage check

A course outline and a standards list become a coverage grid with every gap, repeat, and partial standard flagged.

Adoption review, side by side

Three publishers' samples scored against the adoption rubric, every score quoting the sentence it came from.

PD session follow-through

One session's messy notes become a handout, an exit question, and the follow-up email.

Course catalog, one voice

Descriptions written in a dozen voices rewritten to one format at a family reading level, missing facts flagged.

Wellness

De-identified patterns

An attendance, behavior and wellness export, de-identified on screen, then turned into patterns by time and place.

Survey comments to themes

Hundreds of anonymous comments become ranked themes with counts, a chart, and verbatim quotes.

Community resource guide

A messy agency list becomes a family guide sorted by need, with missing hours and phone numbers flagged.

Teaching, learning and culture — secondary

Data meeting prep

A de-identified data summary becomes a timed agenda and guiding questions for a principals' data meeting.

Directive to building brief

One dense central-office directive becomes a principal's brief and five minutes of staff-meeting talking points.

Tiered intervention templates

Four buildings' different practices become one comparison and blank Tier 1, 2 and 3 forms.

Communications

One message, every family

One district notice at three reading levels, in two tones, in English and Spanish, side by side.

Eight voices, one newsletter

Eight department submissions in eight voices become one newsletter, with conflicts to check before sending.

Holding statement in minutes

Confirmed facts become a holding statement plus the questions reporters will ask next, known and not yet known.

One announcement, every channel

One memo becomes a website post, a social post, and a text alert, each counted against its limit.

Programs and operations

Draft schedule, conflicts found

Rooms, presenters and constraints become a schedule grid and the conflicts that need your decision.

Numbers to report narrative

A program data table becomes grant-report sections; click any sentence to see the numbers behind it.
