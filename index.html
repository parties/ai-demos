AI demos for school and district staff

Each demo walks through one real task from school or district work. You see the prompt a staff member typed, the files they gave the AI, and what the AI wrote back, so you can judge whether it would help with your own work and what you would still need to check.

There are 10 demos here, grouped by job. Every name and number in them is made up, and every page works without an internet connection.

For everyone

  • AI answers that look right but aren't: how to check them

    Three AI answers with mistakes planted in them: a policy summary, a budget total, and a legal question. Find the mistakes, then see the four checking habits that catch them.

Superintendent's office

  • Drafting a board memo from your notes

    Give the AI your rough notes and last month's memo. It drafts this month's memo in the same format and marks every figure you still need to confirm.

  • Practicing for a difficult meeting

    The AI plays a skeptical board member, an upset parent, or a reporter. You answer its questions, and it gives you feedback on each answer.

Human capital and HR

  • Writing a job posting from a duties list

    Give the AI the duties list you already have. It writes a posting in district format and flags jargon and requirements that would turn away qualified applicants, with the reason for each.

Information services

  • Reviewing a vendor's privacy policy

    Give the AI a vendor's privacy policy and the district's privacy checklist. It checks each item and quotes the line it based its answer on. The approval decision stays with you.

Curriculum and instructional technology

  • Checking a course against the standards

    Give the AI a course outline and a standards list. It returns a coverage table showing which standards are missing, repeated, or only partly covered.

Wellness

  • Finding patterns in student data without exposing students

    Start with an attendance, behavior, and wellness export. Remove names and ID numbers first, on screen, then look at the patterns by time and place.

Teaching, learning and culture: secondary

  • Passing a district directive on to building staff

    Give the AI a dense central-office directive. It writes a one-page brief for the principal and five minutes of talking points for the staff meeting.

Communications

  • Adapting one message for every family

    One district notice rewritten at three reading levels, in two tones, in English and Spanish, side by side.

Programs and operations

  • Writing a grant report from program data

    Give the AI a table of program numbers. It drafts the needs, outcomes, and next-steps sections of a grant report. Click any sentence to see the numbers behind it.
