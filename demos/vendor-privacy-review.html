Reviewing a vendor's privacy policy

Before teachers use a new app with students, someone at the district reads the company's privacy policy and compares it with the district's own privacy requirements. Here, the district's information services director gives the AI a vendor's public privacy policy and the district's six-item checklist, and asks it to check each item. For every item, the AI marks it met, not met, or unclear, and quotes the exact sentence it relied on. It then lists other parts of the policy worth noticing and the questions to send the vendor. It does not approve or reject the vendor; that decision stays with the district.
