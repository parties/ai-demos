Practicing for a difficult meeting

Before a hard conversation, it helps to practice your answers out loud. Here, a superintendent asks the AI to play the person on the other side of the table: a skeptical board member, an upset parent, or a local reporter. The AI asks three tough questions, one at a time. After each answer, it steps out of character and coaches like a communications advisor: what worked, what to tighten, and one sentence to try instead.

Choose who you are meeting and the topic, then press "Start the rehearsal" and step through the conversation. The superintendent's answers are already written in, so you can see how the coaching responds to them.

    Before you use it
    
    
    The coaching is a starting point, not a script. The superintendent decides what is true, what is already settled, and what to say. Check every fact and every suggested line against the district's own documents before using it in a real meeting, because the AI's wording can sound more certain than the facts allow.
