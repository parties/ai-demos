Drafting a board memo from your notes

Board memos follow the same format every month, which makes a first draft a good job for AI. Here, a superintendent gives the AI their rough notes and last month's memo, and asks for this month's memo in the same format. The AI fills in what the notes support and marks every figure it could not confirm, so the superintendent edits a draft instead of starting from a blank page.
