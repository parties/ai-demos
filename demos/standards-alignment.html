Checking a course against the standards

Before a department meeting, a department head or curriculum lead often needs to know whether a course outline covers every standard on the state list. Doing it by hand means reading every unit against every standard. Here, the department head gives the AI the standards list and the department's draft outline and asks for a coverage grid. The grid shows which units teach each standard. Below it, the AI lists the standards no unit teaches, the ones taught twice, and one low-cost fix for each.

Tip: let Claude look up the standards for you

        
Finding and pasting the right standards list is often the slowest part of this task. In Claude, the Learning Commons Knowledge Graph connector looks up official K–12 standards for all 50 states. You can ask for "the [state] standards for [grade and course]" instead of attaching the list yourself. It can also break a standard into the smaller skills inside it and show which standards come before and after it, which helps when you plan how to fill a gap. Adding it takes one click and needs no separate sign-in. Compare the list it returns with your state's official document before you rely on it.

Before you use it

    
The department checks every mark against the actual unit plans and assessments before anyone changes the course. The AI sees only the outline, so a standard that is taught but never written down will show up as a gap, and a topic that is listed but rushed will show up as covered.
