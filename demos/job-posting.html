Writing a job posting from a duties list

Most job postings start as a duties list written by the department, full of salary grades, acronyms, and requirements copied from older postings. Here, a human resources staff member gives the AI that list and asks for a posting in the district's format, written for someone who has never worked in a school. The AI rewrites the jargon in plain words. Under each line it also flags any requirement that is stricter than the job needs and any wording that could raise a legal concern, and suggests a rewrite. It does not remove anything on its own; human resources decides every flag.

Choose a job to see a different duties list and the posting that comes back. In the AI's answer, you can switch between the draft with its flags and the version with every suggestion accepted.

Before you use it

    
Human resources reviews every flag, fills in the pay range, and confirms the legal requirements before the posting goes up. The AI never removes a requirement on its own, so a person decides each one.

  


  
Every name, number, and document on this page is made up. The AI's answers were written ahead of time, so the page works without an internet connection.
