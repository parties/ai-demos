AI answers that look right but aren't: how to check them

AI tools write confident, polished answers, and some of those answers are wrong. This page shows three tasks a district staff member might give an AI. Each answer looks finished, but we planted mistakes in every one of them, the same kinds of mistakes AI tools really make.

Pick a task, read the AI's answer, and try to find the problems yourself. Then press "Show the mistakes" to see each one and the checking habit that would have caught it.

    Four habits that catch AI mistakes
    
    
    Each mistake on this page is caught by one of these. The ones that apply to the task you picked light up when you show the mistakes.
    
    
    
      
    Ask for the source"Where exactly does that come from?" Then open it yourself.
    
      
    Compare against the originalRead the answer beside what you gave it, line by line.
    
      
    RecomputeRedo every number it calculated, in a spreadsheet.
    
      
    Check the date"As of when?" Its knowledge stops at a training date.
    
    
    
  
    

  
    
    
    Before you use any AI answer
    
    
    Check every citation, number, and legal claim yourself. A polished answer is not the same as a correct one, and the person who sends it is responsible for it.
