Passing a district directive on to building staff

When central office sends a new directive, each principal has to work out what it means for their building and then explain it to staff. The directive is usually one long paragraph of formal language. Here, a principal gives the AI the directive and asks for two things: a one-page brief the principal can act on, and talking points for a five-minute staff meeting. The AI pulls out the dates and duties and lists the questions the directive leaves unanswered, so the principal knows what to ask central office before talking to staff.
