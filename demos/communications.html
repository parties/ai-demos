Adapting one message for every family

District notices are often written in formal language that many families find hard to read, and some families read Spanish first. Here, someone in the district communications office gives the AI one notice from central office and asks for it rewritten at three reading levels, in the tone and language they choose. They then pick the version that fits each audience and check it before it goes out.

Before you use it

    
A person on staff reads every version against the original notice and edits it before anything is sent. The Spanish versions are a first draft only: a fluent Spanish reader checks and signs off on them before they go to families.
